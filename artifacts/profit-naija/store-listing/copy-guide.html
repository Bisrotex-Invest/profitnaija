Full description · Both stores

Description 1635 / 4000 characters

Simple business accounting and bookkeeping for shops, traders and small businesses in Nigeria.

ProfitNaija helps you keep track of what your business earns, spends, owns and owes. Record everyday transactions and review your sales, expenses, inventory and profit in one place.

RECORD SALES AND EXPENSES
Keep organised records of your business sales and spending, instead of relying on scattered notes.

TRACK YOUR INVENTORY
Record stock purchases and sales, review available quantities and keep an eye on your goods.

MANAGE CUSTOMER AND SUPPLIER BALANCES
See outstanding customer and supplier balances so you can follow up on money owed.

CREATE AND SHARE INVOICES
Prepare invoices for your sales and share them with customers.

UNDERSTAND YOUR BUSINESS RESULTS
Review sales, expenses and profit reports to understand how your business is performing.

KEEP TAX RECORDS ORGANISED
View VAT, withholding tax and estimated personal income tax in Tax Center. Tax estimates and reports do not replace professional tax advice or submit returns for you.

ACCESS YOUR RECORDS ON MOBILE AND WEB
Use your ProfitNaija account on your phone and through the web app. Cloud backup and restore are available on eligible plans.

ACCESS AND SUBSCRIPTIONS
A verified account is required. Subscription access is required after the trial. Features depend on your plan. Internet access is needed for account verification, syncing and cloud backup.

ProfitNaija is a business record-keeping app, not a bank, lender or investment service.
